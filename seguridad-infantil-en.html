Child Safety Standards

Prevention of child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) · «Poás Agua y Fuego» app

Last updated: August 9, 2026

In the «Poás Agua y Fuego» app we maintain a zero-tolerance policy against any form of child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM). These standards describe our commitment and the measures we apply to keep a safe environment.

1. Our commitment

We strictly prohibit the creation, upload, publication, storage, transmission or promotion of any content or conduct involving the sexual abuse or exploitation of minors. Any such content will be removed and reported to the competent authorities.

2. Nature of the app

«Poás Agua y Fuego» is an informational portal about the canton of Poás (Costa Rica). It is not a social network or a messaging platform. User interaction is limited to voluntary forms (for example, community stories, complaints or events), whose content is reviewed by our team before publication.

3. What is prohibited

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) in any format.
  • Contacting, grooming or manipulating minors for sexual purposes.
  • Sextortion, trafficking or any sexual exploitation of minors.
  • Links, text or images that promote or facilitate such conduct.

4. Measures we apply

  • Moderation: all user-submitted content is reviewed before publication; anything violating this policy is rejected or removed.
  • Immediate removal of any detected CSAE material.
  • Reporting to authorities and preservation of evidence as required by law.
  • Blocking of the responsible users.

5. How to report

If you detect content or conduct related to child sexual abuse or exploitation in our app, report it immediately to:

Child safety contact: cesarluis@techcentercr.com

We handle these reports with priority and confidentiality.

In case of an emergency or an ongoing crime, also contact the authorities:

  • Costa Rica — Emergencies: 9-1-1
  • Judicial Investigation Agency (OIJ): 800-8000-645
  • National Children's Trust (PANI): 1147

6. Legal compliance

We comply with applicable Costa Rican law on the protection of children and adolescents, and we cooperate with the competent national and international authorities. We also adhere to Google Play's child safety policies.

7. Contact

For questions about these child safety standards, write to cesarluis@techcentercr.com.
