🌋 Must-see

Natural Attractions of the Canton

The destinations that make Poás one of the most stunning natural corners of Costa Rica. Start here.

🌋

Poás Volcano National Park

One of the most visited volcanoes in the country. Its active crater, over a kilometer wide, is a unique sight reached by an easy trail from the visitor center.

Advance booking at SINAC
💧

Botos Lagoon

A volcanic lagoon surrounded by cloud forest inside the national park. Reached via the Botos Trail, a calm walk among mosses, ferns and birds.

Trail inside the park
🏞️

Fraijanes Lagoon Recreational Park

An ideal spot for the whole family: lagoon, green areas, trails, picnic zones and fresh mountain air. Perfect for a stop before or after the volcano.

Family-friendly
🌿

Sabana Redonda Cloud Forest

The volcano's upper slopes are covered in cloud forest, home to quetzals, hummingbirds and lush vegetation. Ideal for birdwatching and nature photography.

Birdwatching
🚴

Cycling Route to the Volcano

The climb to the volcano is a classic among cyclists nationwide for its demanding ascent and Central Valley views. A sporting challenge with a panoramic reward.

Sport & adventure
🍓

Strawberry Farms & Stands

Along the road to the volcano you'll find farms and stands selling fresh strawberries, smoothies and artisanal jams straight from the local grower.

Local product
🎒 Experiences

Experiences & Tours

Activities to experience the canton beyond the view: coffee, nature and tradition.

☕

Coffee Tour at the Poás Farms

Walk the canton's high-altitude coffee plantations and discover the full bean process, from planting to cup. Several local farms offer guided tours and tastings.

🥾

Hiking & Cloud Forest

Natural trails through high-altitude vegetation, with viewpoints over the Central Valley and sightings of endemic flora and fauna. Ideal for short or half-day walks.

🌅

Sunsets in Fraijanes and Poasito

The area's viewpoints and terraces offer some of the best sunset views over the lights of the Central Valley. A must at dusk.

🧀

Palmito Cheese Route

Visit the artisanal cheese makers of Poasito, where the famous palmito cheese is made. Many stands let you watch the process and buy freshly made product.

📸 Viewpoints

Viewpoints & Photo Spots

Quick stops to capture the canton's landscapes.

🌄 Fraijanes Viewpoint

Panoramic view of the Central Valley surrounded by forest and fresh air. Perfect for photos and a break along the way.

🌋 Poás Volcano Crater

The main crater viewpoint, one of the most impressive and accessible volcanic landscapes in the country.

🌆 Poasito Viewpoints

Several spots along the road to the volcano with open views, ideal for sunset.

📝 Tips

Tips for Visitors

The weather in Poás is variable and cold. Plan your visit to make the most of it.

🎟️

Mandatory Volcano Booking

Tickets to Poás Volcano National Park are purchased in advance only on the official SINAC website. They are not sold at the entrance.

🌅

Arrive Early

Book for the early morning. The crater tends to cloud over as the day goes on, so between 8:00 and 8:30 a.m. you have the best chance of seeing it clear.

🧥

Dress Warm & Bring Rain Gear

Temperatures drop sharply at the summit and it can rain without warning. Layered clothing, a jacket and a raincoat are essential year-round.

📷

Check the Live Weather

Search "Poás Crater Webcam" on Google to see live whether the volcano is clear before heading up and plan your day better.
