Our Water Heritage

The Poás massif is not only a giant of fire — it is the humid lung of the region. The cloud forest acts as a giant sponge, capturing moisture and feeding underground water reserves.

  • 💧 Critical recharge area: The Poás massif functions as a "water tower", allowing filtration that feeds the Colima and Barva aquifers, the main drinking water sources for the Greater Metropolitan Area.
  • 🌲 Unique ecosystem that regulates the local climate.
  • 🚰 Direct source for local community water systems (ASADAS).
