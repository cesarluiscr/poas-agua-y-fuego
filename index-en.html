Our Water Heritage

The Poás massif is not only a giant of fire — it is the humid lung of the region. Its cloud forest captures moisture and feeds the reserves that supply millions of people.

🏔️

Water Tower

The massif feeds the Poás, Colima and Barva aquifers, the main drinking water sources for the Greater Metropolitan Area.

🌲

Unique Ecosystem

The cloud forest acts as a giant sponge, regulating the local climate and maintaining the canton's biodiversity.

🚰

Community Aqueducts

Direct source for 9 ASADAS that provide drinking water to all communities in Poás canton.
