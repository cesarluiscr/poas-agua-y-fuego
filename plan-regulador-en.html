Canton Zoning Plan

The planning instrument that guides the sustainable development of Poás canton.

← Back to Home

The Zoning Plan is the local planning instrument that defines land use and development regulations in our canton. Its goal is to ensure orderly and sustainable growth, protecting our water recharge zones and managing volcanic risk.

🏗️

Zoning

Definition of residential, commercial, industrial and protected areas for an orderly use of land.

🌿

Environmental Protection

Protection of forests, water springs and biological corridors essential to local biodiversity.

🌋

Volcanic Risk Management

Special regulations for areas under direct influence of Poás Volcano and its restriction zones.

💧

Water Heritage

Protection of aquifers, recharge zones and rivers as strategic resources for the canton.

🏘️

Urban Development

Regulation of buildings, setbacks, heights and densities to guarantee quality of life.

If you are interested in participating as a citizen in the follow-up of the zoning plan, contact us.
